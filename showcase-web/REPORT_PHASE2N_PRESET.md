# Phase 2N: built-in female preset candidate (Ara 3D Gen)

**Verdict: PRESET CANDIDATE APPROVED FOR REVIEW**, with explicit reservations:
- **Requirements met:** it is a real, licence-clean, editable 3D character with working facial morph
  targets, eyes, teeth, tongue and a rig.
- **Not met:** the *preferred* photorealistic, macro-close-up quality. It is a game-quality MakeHuman
  character.
- **Must be fixed before the Studio uses it:**
  - the eye-bone pivots are 3.8 mm off the eyeball centres;
  - mouth asymmetry, cheek puff, lip bite and tongue-out need new shapes.

**Scope:** nothing in the Studio changed, Play stays disabled, nothing was pushed, and the
competition release and ZIP are untouched. The 3D Scan Store scan and the old male MakeHuman model
were not used.

**Inspected:**
- **Read clearly:** brows (surprise, sadness, anger), smile and laugh, frown, both winks (on the correct
  sides), pucker, and jaw open.
- **Subtle:** nose wrinkle and the confusion brows.
- **Weak:** the fear mouth.
- **Mouth close-up:** the lower teeth and tongue move with the jaw. The **upper teeth are not visible**
  in the open-mouth close-up, and the teeth look grey and plastic.
- **Overall look:** game-like rather than photographic:
  - flat skin (no normal map);
  - stylised irises;
  - a hard hair-cap edge on the forehead;
  - card brows and hair;
  - a pendant chain floating above the skin.

## 9. Known limitations and required work before adoption

1. **Visual quality is below "photorealistic, macro close-up".** There is no skin normal map, the
   skin colour is 2K, the brows, lashes and hair are cards, and the eyes are stylised. It's suitable
   for mid shots, not macro.
2. **Eye-bone pivots are 3.8 mm off centre;** this must be fixed before any gaze.
3. **Mouth shapes are symmetric only;** 7 expressions need new shapes, and crying needs tears.
4. **One merged mesh** includes the full body and outfit. It is heavy for a face page (152k
   triangles, 12.8 MB). A head-and-shoulders trim or LOD would help.
5. **Upper teeth are hidden** in the open-mouth view, and the teeth shading looks artificial.
6. **The preset was altered** (sunglasses and handbag removed); that's documented.
7. **Only 12 of 60 mappings** were visually verified.
