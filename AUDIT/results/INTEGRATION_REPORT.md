## 4. Root cause of the pupil-centre discrepancy (WS1)

Three distinct pupil definitions (`research_pupil_levels.json`): **A** anatomical pupil of the wrapper (3-D, its straight projection, and the analytic-cornea apparent point = official `pupil_center_2D`); **B** the pupil actually painted on the rendered eyeball (mesh + texture); **C** the image-only detector output (a prediction, never ground truth).

Mesh + texture measurement (no detector, no fit; `ws1_depth/depth_probe_extended.json → reset_pose`):

| Eye | wrapper pupil depth (mm) | painted pupil on mesh, along gaze axis (mm) | painted pupil lateral offset (world mm, x/y/z) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L | 14.187 | 16.279 | [0.1613, -0.0139, -0.3426] |
| R | 14.291 | 16.348 | [0.1572, 0.0135, -0.3418] |

Effective fitted depth (`ws1_depth/depth_fit.json`; effective fitted parameter of the rendered asset as seen by the detector; NOT an anatomical dimension). Model: detected = project(eye_centre + q_gaze @ (axis * d)) + constant offset (dx, dy) per eye and condition; unrefracted pinhole projection. Selection: d* = argmin over the depth grid of the combined x/y residual RMSE of the fit set, offset re-fitted at every d. Bootstrap: non-parametric percentile bootstrap, 2000 resamples, numpy default_rng seed 12345; sampling unit = fit pose (one eye's detected pupil at one gaze, its x and y kept paired), drawn with replacement from that eye's usable fit poses; offset and d* re-fitted per resample; interval = 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles of d* (grid-quantised to the 0.05 mm step); eyes fitted separately. Sensitivity: leave-one-pose-out d* for every fit pose; left and right eyes at the same gaze share rendering/lighting so they are reported separately, never pooled. held-out poses (ids 20..33) enter neither the depth selection, the offset, nor the bootstrap; they are only predicted.

Per-pose held-out errors (dx, dy, Euclidean) for every model are in `ws1_depth/depth_fit.json → conditions.<c>.<eye>.heldout_*.per_pose`; raw detections in `ws1_depth/depth_fit_raw.csv`; held-out pose identities in `heldout_pose_inputs`.

Scratch baseline (`ws1_depth_baseline_scratch/`) vs clean run: no_lids_lens.L: heldout_wrapper_gt_no_offset.rmse_px 3.136→3.137; no_lids_lens.R: identical; full.L: identical; full.R: identical

**Conclusion.** The official 2-D GT places the pupil at the anatomical depth (~14.2–14.3 mm from the eye centre) and refracts it through an analytic cornea. The asset paints its pupil on the outer eyeball surface; the mesh puts it ~2 mm further out and slightly below/aside the anatomical axis, and a single effective depth plus a constant offset predicts the held-out detections far better than the official GT (table above). This is a GT-definition / asset mismatch, not a rotation or projection error (compliance §8).

**Cornea, separately.** The scene's `Lens` material is Transparent + Glossy (Fresnel only), so the render does not refract. The official `pupil_center_2D` includes analytic refraction; `pupil_center_2D_unrefracted` does not. WS3: rendered pupil masks with and without the Lens mesh have IoU 1.0 (mean).
